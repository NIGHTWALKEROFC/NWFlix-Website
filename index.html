    What it does

    • Finds your movies. It reads the videos in your own Telegram account. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
    • Plays them straight away. Watch while it loads, or download a movie to keep it on your phone.
    • Remembers where you stopped. Pick up a movie again from the same minute.
    • Plays most formats. A second player is built in for files that the normal one can't open.

    How to install

    1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
    2. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser. This is a one-time question.
    3. Open NWFlix and sign in with your Telegram number.

    You need Android 7 or newer. Once installed, the app tells you when a new version is ready and updates itself.
