Your Telegram movies,

NWFlix looks through the chats and channels you already have in Telegram, finds the movies and series, and shows them with posters, ratings and details. Tap one to watch.

Free · Android 7 or newer

What it does

  • Finds your movies. It reads the videos in your own Telegram account. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Plays them straight away. Watch while it loads, or download a movie to keep it on your phone.
  • Remembers where you stopped. Pick up a movie again from the same minute.
  • Plays most formats. A second player is built in for files that the normal one can't open.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android. We pick the right file for your phone.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser (one time).
  3. Open NWFlix and sign in with your Telegram number.

NWFlix does not contain any movies. It only shows the files that are already in your Telegram. Use it only with content you are allowed to watch.
